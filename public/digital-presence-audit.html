Twelve areas, one monthly report

Every report breaks your digital presence into the parts that actually move new-customer enquiries — not a wall of screenshots. Built the same way whether you run a clinic, a café, or a salon.

Reviews Health

Star rating, response rate, and unanswered negatives — plus what customers actually praise or criticise, pulled straight from your review text.

AREA 07

Side-by-side comparison against the businesses actually competing with you nearby, plus a content gap map of what they post that you don't.

HOW IT WORKS

Five weighted signals in, one comparable score out

Each signal is measured independently, then weighted into a single composite out of 100 — so a business with a great star rating but a dead social account, or a broken booking link, doesn't get an inflated score.
