HOW THE SCORE IS CALCULATED

Four weighted categories in, one comparable score out

Each category is measured independently, then weighted into a single composite out of 100 — so a business with a great star rating but weak AI visibility, or a broken booking link, doesn't get an inflated score. AI & search visibility now feeds the score directly, not just a descriptive callout.
